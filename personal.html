Hobbies and Interests

Rock Climbing

I've been rock climbing for about five years now. I started climbing in a local gym, then moved to outdoor climbing about three years ago. I mostly climb in the Alberta Rockies; however, I have also climbed at a few locations in Tahoe and Yosemite! Climbing for me has been a great opportunity to explore the outdoors and see places I never would be able to reach just by hiking.

Snowboarding

Snowboarding has been part of my life since I was a young kid. I was lucky to grow up in a cold, snowy city with three small ski resorts within the city limits. I first started to learn during school field trips to the local hills, and from there, it clicked. The -20 degree weather, five-hour-long drives, and sleepless nights are always worth a good day of snowboarding.

Hiking

I didn't grow up being outdoorsy. I went on my first hike while in university, wearing jeans and Converse, carrying a single 500-ml water bottle. Since then, I've learned a couple of things and have developed an appreciation for the outdoors. By far, my favorite adventures have included a three-day backpacking trip on the Alta Via 1 in Italy. However, the trail I believe everyone should experience once would be the Lake O'Hara Alpine Circuit in British Columbia. Lake O'Hara has some of the best trails I have ever seen. But because of the high demand, it needs to be planned months in advance due to limited bus space!

Baking

I started to get very good at cake decorating when I worked at a cake shop for about three years in high school. Since then, I have always enjoyed it because I loved sharing the things I made with my friends and family. Baking things from scratch also means it's healthy because I didn't buy it from a store (haha). Mostly, I love baking cheesecake and cheesecake cupcakes. But recently I have been getting into baking bread!
